    Why the mask matters

    Without a mask, an edit model is free to redraw the whole picture. With one, only the painted area is replaced and everything else stays pixel-for-pixel. That is the difference between "an image like mine" and "my image, changed here".

    Styles, variants and speed

    Create can apply any of Venice's 76 style presets, ask for up to 4 variants in one call so you can pick the best, and turn on web search or prompt enhancement. The fast toggle skips the prompt-optimisation pass when you want a result quickly.

    Models + pricing

    All models come from the live Venice catalog. Create lists text-to-image models; Edit and Combine list only Venice's image-edit models. Prices are shown in the model picker.

    Leaving and coming back

    Switch apps, lock the phone, close the tab — it is fine. In-flight work is written down before it starts and your results are stored on this device, so reopening vivMorph puts you back where you were with the image still there. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except to Venice to make the image.
